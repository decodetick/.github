## rozkoduj

Open-source trading tools, AI agents & automation for financial markets.

We research, build and ship tools that make algorithmic trading and market analysis accessible to everyone — from MCP servers for AI assistants to backtesting frameworks and trading automation.

### What we're working on

- Market data & analysis tools (MCP servers, screeners, indicators)
- Trading journal & portfolio tracking with behavioral analysis
- Backtesting & strategy development frameworks
- Research on the finance/trading tooling ecosystem

### Get involved

We're just getting started. Star our repos, open issues, or contribute.
